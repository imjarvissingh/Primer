# Primer — YC Founder Video Outline (public-safe template)

Based on YC's official page (https://www.ycombinator.com/video/, accessed 2026-10-16): **about 1 minute, only the founder(s) talking**, introduce yourself, "explain what you're doing and why", add anything else about the founders or project, all founders appear, **no demo or promotional footage** (the product demo has its own field), and "do not recite a written script: use bullet points instead". File format/size limits were not stated on the pages I could read: check the portal (a secondhand report mentioned 100 MB; unverified).

This is **not** the product demo ([YC-DEMO-STORYBOARD.md](YC-DEMO-STORYBOARD.md)). Nothing is recorded or uploaded by this repo. Every `[FOUNDER: …]` field is yours; the assistant does not know your history and must not fill it in.

## Recording checklist

- [ ] Face the light; quiet room; phone or laptop camera at eye level; check audio first
- [ ] 3–5 bullet notes off-screen, no teleprompter
- [ ] Do one run for timing, then record; 2–3 takes max
- [ ] Under 60–70 s; trim dead air only
- [ ] No product footage, slides or music
- [ ] Co-founders (if any) appear: [FOUNDER: yes/no]
- [ ] Check the file against the portal's format and size limits
- [ ] Don't upload until `YC-SUBMISSION-CHECKLIST.md` review gates pass

## Pitfalls

Reading a script; vague hype ("revolutionize education"); claiming results or users you don't have; putting a demo in this video; overstating AI-built code as hand-written (or hiding it); a child's name, face, or any real child's data on screen; rambling past one minute.
